### Project Description
[Dog Poop Stock Exchange is a fictional financial market that transforms dogs into publicly traded assets and simulates an interactive stock exchange entirely through a local-hosted web application. Users can trade virtual shares, monitor dynamically changing prices, analyze fictional financial metrics, follow market news, and experience a completely unnecessary canine economy.]

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
[Every day, dogs produce millions of kilograms of completely useless biological assets. Nobody tracks their market value. Nobody trades them. Nobody knows whether Bruno is bullish or bearish. This project exists to correct this catastrophic failure of the global financial system.

Of course, dog poop has absolutely no real market value, the money is completely fictional, dogs obviously don't own companies, and none of this solves an actual problem.]


### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
[We created a completely unnecessary fictional stock exchange where dogs are treated like publicly traded companies. Each dog receives a stock price that changes dynamically according to completely ridiculous events such as cats, treats, naps, vacuum cleaners, barking incidents, park visits, and unexpected poop production.

Users receive virtual money and can buy and sell dog stocks, track their portfolio, read fake breaking news, manipulate the fictional market, monitor the Poop Index, unlock achievements, compete on a leaderboard, and receive completely unnecessary financial advice from a fake AI advisor.
